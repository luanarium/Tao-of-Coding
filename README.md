# Tao-of-Coding
> My coding philosophy

<details>
  <summary>Limitations are design choices. Everything doing one thing is the safe, minimalist, boring approach.</summary>

> Album art: the first time I saw it in a player was in iTunes/iPod. Before that, no album art at all. Limitations are rarely because of a hard reason. Even less so now. 

> The most idiomatic way of adding album art to audio is to make a video.

> Limitations of an app today are nothing more than limitations on design and vision, not anything real. Practically every device has a web browser.

> ID3 tags existed in the 1990s. Album art wasn’t in early players because developers didn’t prioritize it, not because it was impossible.

</details>
